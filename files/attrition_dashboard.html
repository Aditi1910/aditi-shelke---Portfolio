Risk is front-loaded

Attrition rate by years at the company — year one does most of the damage
The finding: attrition is not spread evenly and it is not about pay. It concentrates in people working overtime, in Sales, in their first year. Fix the workload and the onboarding for that one overlapping group and you address roughly half of all exits without touching the salary bill.
Data: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance, a public practice dataset of 1,470 records. Headline figures shown as published: 16.1% overall attrition, 20.6% / 19.0% / 13.8% by department, 30.5% versus 10.4% by overtime status.
