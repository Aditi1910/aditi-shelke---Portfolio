Delay reasons

SHARE OF ALL LATE SHIPMENTS · PARETO
The problem is variance, not speed. Average lead time of 18.6 days is workable; a swing of ±6.2 days is not, and it is what forces $14.2M of inventory to sit as buffer. Two suppliers, Meridian and Northgate, generate 54% of all late shipments while being the two cheapest on unit price. Dual-source those two lanes and hold them to a variance SLA — that releases roughly $3.1M of safety stock without touching service levels.
