The finding: Eco Plus is the only cabin that scores below the one beneath it — 44% satisfied against Economy's 51%. The upsell sells legroom, but legroom ranks fifth among the attributes that actually move satisfaction. Online boarding and seat comfort do, and in Eco Plus both stay at Economy level. The cabin is priced like a premium and delivered like a bulkhead row.
DATA · Airline Passenger Satisfaction, a public practice dataset of 103,904 surveyed passengers. Cabin-level satisfaction shown as analysed in the accompanying project report: 78% Business, 51% Eco, 44% Eco Plus. Attribute ratings are the survey's own 1–5 service scores.
