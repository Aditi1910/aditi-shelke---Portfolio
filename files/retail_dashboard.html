The leak is not revenue, it is policy. Tables alone destroy $17.7K of profit, and any order discounted past ~22% is written at a loss. Central discounts at 24%, nearly double the West, and earns roughly half the margin. Cap discounts at 20%, re-price or retire the three loss-making lines, and hold Central to West's discount discipline — that is an estimated +20–30% profit on flat revenue, without selling a single extra unit.
