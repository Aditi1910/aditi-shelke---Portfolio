Property risk indicators
Every listing comes with a plain-language risk summary
Clear title
No pending litigation, no encumbrances on record. Bank has clear possession rights. Lower legal risk.
Symbolic possession
Bank has legal rights but property may be occupied. Eviction process required post-purchase. Proceed with legal advice.
Court stay risk
Borrower has filed an appeal. Auction could be stayed by court order. High risk — independent legal review essential.
