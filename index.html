// 01 · About

Finance background.
Analytics mind.
Storyteller at heart.

I turn messy, multi-source data into the answers that decide what a business, a product, or a campaign does next. Four-plus years in financial services plus an MS in Business Analytics gave me the mix most teams are missing: analytical depth and real domain fluency.

My work lives where data, statistics, and strategy meet. I don't stop at the dashboard. I dig into why a number moved, what it signals for the coming quarter, and what a team should actually do with it.

From credit risk models across 50+ accounts to a profitability diagnostic that found where a $2.3M business was leaking margin, every project starts from the same place: what should someone do differently because of this?

That range is deliberate. I've built credit-risk models inside regulated finance, turned manual reporting into self-serve systems in operations, and most recently sized an untapped market and built the cost-and-emissions business case for an aviation-automation venture. Different industries, one throughline: get to the insight that changes what happens next.
