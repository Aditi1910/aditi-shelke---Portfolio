Where deals stall

DAYS SPENT IN EACH STAGE · DOT = MEDIAN
The quarter did not lose deals, it postponed them. Win rate held at 24.6% and deal size grew 12%, but the cycle stretched 11 days and $2.1M slipped into Q1 — almost exactly the amount missing from plan. The stall is concentrated in one stage: Security Review, where mid-market deals sit for a median 26 days, four times longer than any other step. Pre-stage security documentation at qualification and most of that $2.1M lands in the quarter it was forecast for.
